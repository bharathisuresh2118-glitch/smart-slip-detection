OUR SOLUTION

Detection that goes beyond vibration.

The Smart Slip Detection and Alert System uses an MPU6050 accelerometer and gyroscope to monitor movement and detect significant changes associated with sudden impact.

Instead of treating every vibration as an emergency, the system checks movement and stability after an impact before confirming a possible slip or fall.

THE SCIENCE

Turning movement into meaningful data.

The system uses physics and sensor measurements to identify sudden changes in movement and evaluate whether they could indicate a possible slip or fall.

01 ACCELEROMETER
X
Y
Z
MPU

Acceleration

The accelerometer measures changes in acceleration along the X, Y, and Z axes. A sudden change can indicate that a significant movement or impact has occurred.

Acceleration a = Δv / Δt
02 GYROSCOPE
↻

Rotation & Orientation

The gyroscope measures rotational movement. This helps the system observe changes in the sensor's orientation and movement.

03 IMPACT ANALYSIS
NORMAL IMPACT AFTER EVENT

Detecting Significant Impact

The system looks for a sudden, significant change rather than reacting to every small vibration.

04 STABILITY
MOVEMENT CHECK

Movement & Stability

After an impact is detected, the system checks subsequent movement and stability before deciding whether the event should be treated as a possible emergency.

∑
DETECTION PRINCIPLE

One sensor reading isn't the whole story.

The project combines impact information with movement and stability checks. This approach is intended to reduce false alarms caused by ordinary vibration or minor movement.

IMPACT + MOVEMENT + STABILITY → POSSIBLE FALL
